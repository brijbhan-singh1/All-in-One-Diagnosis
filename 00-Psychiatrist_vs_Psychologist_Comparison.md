Do you mean psychiatrist vs psychologist? They are different mental health professionals.

## Psychiatrist vs Psychologist

Psichiatria: cos'è e di cosa si occupa lo psichiatra - The Wom Healthy

## 1. Psychiatrist

- A medical doctor who specializes in mental health.
- Can prescribe medicines.
- Treats conditions such as depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, and schizophrenia.
- May use medication and other treatments.

How To Become A Clinical Psychologist

## 2. Psychologist

- Specializes in thoughts, emotions, and behaviour.
- Usually provides therapy and psychological counselling.
- Helps with stress, anxiety, relationship problems, and emotional difficulties.
- Generally does not prescribe medicines in India.

## Whom should you visit?

| Your concern | Who may help |
| --- | --- |
| Stress or relationship problems | Psychologist |
| Overthinking or emotional difficulties | Psychologist |
| Anxiety or panic attacks | Either |
| Severe depression or suicidal thoughts | Psychiatrist or urgent mental health service |
| Mental health symptoms that may require medication | Psychiatrist |

Remember: You can see both. A psychiatrist can manage medication, while a psychologist can provide ongoing therapy.
